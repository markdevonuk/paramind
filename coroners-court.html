Coroner's Court Visit

An exclusive session for SWAST staff, hosted in partnership with HM Coroner's Service.

About the Session

Join us for a visit to a Coroner's Court and a talk from Philip Spinney, His Majesty's Senior Coroner for the County of Devon, Plymouth and Torbay coroner area, together with a Coroner's investigator.

The purpose of the session is to:

  • Encourage closer working relationships with SWAST
  • Understand what coroners really need from us in our paperwork
  • Learn what happens if we get called to court

There will be a Q&A session too. Sessions last approximately 2 hours.

These are the only two dates available and, due to pressure on the Coroners, it is unlikely this event will be repeated. Places are strictly limited.
