Alignment with UK Paramedic Standards

For universities, training providers and ambulance trusts

Educational alignment only. These documents describe how Paramind supports learning against the UK frameworks. They do not constitute clinical guidance.

Paramind has been deliberately mapped against the three principal frameworks that shape UK paramedic education, registration and practice:

  • The HCPC Standards of Proficiency — Paramedics (effective 1 September 2023)
  • The Royal College of Paramedics Paramedic Curriculum, 6th Edition (April 2024)
  • The Paramedic Apprenticeship Standard (ST0567, version 2.0, Level 6)

Because the curriculum is designed to align with the HCPC standards and the apprenticeship is HCPC-regulated, the three mappings describe the same platform from three complementary angles. Each is provided in full below, alongside a single overview that brings them together.

What Paramind is — and what it is not

Paramind is a supplementary educational and continuing professional development (CPD) tool. It complements — it does not replace — approved education, supervised clinical practice, or an employer's training and governance. It does not assess, certify or sign off proficiency or competence against any standard; that remains a matter for the registrant, their approved programme, their End-Point Assessment, or their employer.

By deliberate policy, Paramind generates no drug names, doses or treatment protocols. Clinical detail is left to JRCALC and local trust guidance, and all clinical content is positioned as revision and learning material. The mappings are transparent about scope: where an element is developed and assessed in person, or sits outside what a digital tool can do, this is stated plainly rather than over-claimed.
