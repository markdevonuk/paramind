Questions you definitely have 🤔

Will my idea actually get built?

Maybe! No promises — but ideas that come up repeatedly or have a clear learning benefit tend to jump the queue. Think of it like triage. The good ones get the resus bay.

Can I suggest something completely wild?

Absolutely. The Bone Lab came from someone suggesting "what if there were a skeleton you could click on" — which sounded ridiculous and turned out to be brilliant. Shoot your shot.

What if my idea already exists in Paramind?

Embarrassing for you, but we won't judge. We'll just reply with a very polite "have you tried the app?" and point you in the right direction.

How long until I hear back?

We aim to acknowledge great ideas personally. If you don't hear back, it doesn't mean we ignored it — it means we've added it to the list and Mark has forgotten to reply. Classic.
