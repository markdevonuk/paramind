A paramedic game that tests real assessment skills

Clue Hunt is a paramedic game from Paramind for student paramedics, newly qualified paramedics and anyone who wants to sharpen their patient assessment. You arrive on scene, take a doorway look, then examine an illustrated patient from head to toe using an ABCDE approach.

Count the breathing rate and pulse yourself, listen to the chest, press on the ankles, check the capillary refill and ask the right questions. When you’re ready, make your working impression. The debrief shows every clue you found and missed, and Hollie explains why each sign appears.
