🫀 ECG Rhythms

Choose a rhythm to see it on the monitor, as a 12-lead and inside the heart.

For educational purposes only - not for clinical decision-making

The monitor, heart view and drawn 12-leads are simplified teaching examples. Real-world 12-lead examples are recorded ECGs from PTB-XL and the PhysioNet/CinC Challenge 2021 (PhysioNet, CC BY 4.0). Real ECGs can vary significantly across leads, patients and clinical contexts. Deeper learning and supervised clinical practice are essential for safe ECG interpretation.
