Paramedic AI tools are artificial intelligence-powered applications designed to support paramedic education and clinical learning. Paramind specifically provides trust-specific guidance based on your ambulance service's protocols. They're ideal for student paramedics learning clinical decision-making, NQPs building confidence, and experienced paramedics seeking quick reference information.

The trust-specific guidance ensures you're learning protocols relevant to your chosen ambulance service.

Paramind supports all 15 UK ambulance services: EEAST, EMAS, NEAS, NIAS, NWAS, SAS, SCAS, SECAmb, SWAST, WAST, WMAS, YAS, as well as SJA (St John Ambulance), JAS (Jersey Ambulance Service), and BHL (Bristow Group). When you register, you select your trust and all AI guidance is tailored to that service's specific protocols.

Our AI tools are designed to support learning and revision, not replace clinical judgment.

Paramind provides CPD certificates for completed learning scenarios which can be used to evidence your continuous professional development activities. These certificates document your learning hours and topics covered, which you can include in your CPD portfolio for HCPC registration renewal.

Educational Tool Disclaimer

Paramind is an educational support tool and is not a substitute for clinical judgment. Users remain fully responsible for their clinical decisions. Always verify critical information against official sources and follow your trust's protocols. In emergencies, seek senior clinical advice. The JRCalc references are not the very latest so you should use your JRCalc app to find up to date guidance.
