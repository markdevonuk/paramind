Before You Begin
You are about to enter a simulated cardiac arrest scenario. This is designed to put you under real pressure — because that's exactly the point.

You may feel frightened, overwhelmed, or not ready to face this on the road. That feeling is completely normal. It means the simulation is doing its job.

Take a breath. Think about your training. Work through it steadily.

These patients are not real. No one gets hurt here. Every mistake is a learning opportunity, not a consequence.
