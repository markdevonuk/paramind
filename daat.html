Critical Care Cars, Exeter & Eaglescott
No public feed
The cars don't broadcast a public position, so their status can't be shown here. They typically respond when weather or maintenance grounds the aircraft, or when road is quicker.

How to read this

  • Airborne: the aircraft is transmitting and flying, so it is probably on a mission or returning.
  • On ground: transmitting while landed. At base it is likely ready; away from base it is likely at scene or a hospital.
  • Not seen: no signal. Usually means it is shut down at base, but low flying in valleys can also drop out of receiver coverage.
  • This is not official availability. Crew status, weather limits and maintenance aren't visible in transponder data.
