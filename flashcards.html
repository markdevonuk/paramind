Smart revision, not just repetition
read why

Paramind uses a spaced repetition algorithm to make your revision as efficient as possible. Every card you see is scored based on how well you knew it — Got it, Kind of, or No idea. The app uses that score to decide when you need to see that card again.

Cards you know well get spaced further and further apart. Cards you struggle with come back sooner. Over time, you spend your revision time exactly where it's needed — not going over things you already know.

This is the same method used by Anki, trusted by medical students worldwide.
