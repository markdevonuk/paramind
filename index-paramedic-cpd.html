What is CPD?

Continuing Professional Development (CPD) is the ongoing learning you do throughout your career to maintain and improve your knowledge and skills. As a registered paramedic, the HCPC requires you to keep a portfolio demonstrating your continued professional development.

CPD isn't just about ticking boxes – it's about genuinely improving your practice and providing better care for your patients. The best CPD is learning that directly impacts how you work.

HCPC Requirements

The HCPC doesn't require a specific number of CPD hours. Instead, they want to see that your CPD activities have contributed to the quality of your practice. You need to be able to explain what you learned, why it was relevant, and how it improved your work.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CPD for paramedics?

CPD (Continuing Professional Development) is the ongoing learning and development that paramedics undertake to maintain and improve their skills. The HCPC requires paramedics to maintain a CPD portfolio demonstrating continued learning to remain registered.

How many CPD hours do paramedics need?

The HCPC doesn't specify a set number of hours. Instead, they require you to demonstrate that your CPD activities have contributed to the quality of your practice. Focus on meaningful learning that improves patient care rather than simply counting hours.

Are Paramind CPD certificates accepted by the HCPC?

Paramind certificates document your learning activities and can be included in your CPD portfolio as evidence of self-directed learning. The HCPC accepts a wide range of CPD evidence including online learning, as long as you can demonstrate how it improved your practice. We recommend combining certificates with written reflection for the strongest evidence.

Can I use Paramind for trust mandatory training?

Paramind is designed for self-directed CPD rather than mandatory trust training. However, many users find it valuable for reinforcing topics covered in mandatory training and for additional professional development beyond trust requirements.

How do I generate a CPD certificate?

Paramind Pro users can generate certificates from the CPD Portfolio section of the app. After completing a learning activity (like a scenario or topic exploration), you can generate and download a certificate documenting what you covered.
