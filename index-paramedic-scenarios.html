What Are Paramedic Scenarios?

Scenarios are simulated patient encounters that let you practice your clinical skills without real-world consequences. They're a core part of paramedic training – used in universities, OSCEs, and continuing professional development.

Traditionally, scenarios require instructors, actors, and equipment. Paramind brings scenario practice to your phone or laptop, available whenever you want to learn – powered by AI that responds realistically to your questions and actions.

Student Mode – More guidance, helpful prompts
Paramedic Mode – Harder, less cooperative patients

Frequently Asked Questions

What are paramedic scenarios?

Paramedic scenarios are simulated patient encounters used for training and assessment. They allow paramedics and students to practice clinical decision-making, patient assessment, and treatment in a safe environment without putting real patients at risk.

How realistic are Paramind scenarios?

Our scenarios are designed by experienced paramedics to reflect real pre-hospital presentations. The AI responds dynamically to your questions and actions – ask about pain, and the patient describes it; request observations, and you get realistic vitals. Patients in "Paramedic Mode" can be uncooperative, confused, or poor historians – just like real life!

Can I use scenarios to prepare for OSCEs?

Absolutely. Scenarios help you practice the systematic approach examiners look for: scene safety, primary survey, focused history, examination, working impression, treatment, and handover. Many students use Paramind specifically for OSCE preparation.

Are scenarios included in the free plan?

The free plan includes a limited preview of scenarios so you can try them out. For full access to all scenario categories and unlimited practice, you'll need Paramind Pro at £4.99/month.

What's the difference between Student and Paramedic mode?

Student Mode provides more guidance and helpful prompts throughout the scenario – great for building foundational skills. Paramedic Mode is harder: patients may be less cooperative, give vague histories, or have multiple problems. It's designed to challenge experienced clinicians.

Can I earn CPD from scenarios?

Yes! Paramind Pro users can generate CPD certificates for completed learning scenarios. These document the clinical topics covered and can be added to your HCPC portfolio as evidence of reflective learning.
