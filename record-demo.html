paramind

Write better ePCRs

Role-play a patient interaction with a colleague. Paramind listens, then writes it up as a structured record using the medical model, so you can see what a clear, complete ePCR looks like and spot what you missed.

How it works
  1. Role-play the callOne of you is the clinician, the other the patient. Up to 20 minutes.
  2. Paramind listensYour conversation is transcribed a minute at a time while you talk.
  3. Get your recordPress Finish and it's written up in the medical model, with other conditions to consider.
The medical model
PCPresenting condition: why the patient called, in a few words
HPCHistory of presenting condition: onset, duration, character, what makes it better or worse, associated symptoms
PMHPast medical history
DHDrug history: regular and recent medications
AllergiesAny known allergies
OAOn arrival: what you saw and heard as you walked in
OEOn examination: abnormal observations, investigations and findings
IMPYour working impression
DIFFOther conditions to consider
TTreatment given
PPlan: convey, discharge on scene, referral
Get the most from it
  • Say abnormal observations out loud. Normal observations aren't written up, because your equipment records them.
  • Say your working impression and plan out loud, as you would at handover.
  • Look for "Not stated". It shows something that wasn't covered, which is a prompt for what to ask next time.
  • Always check the record. Speech-to-text can mishear, and the record is only as good as what was said.
Before you start
  • Simulated role-play only. Never record a real patient, and never use real names, addresses, dates of birth or any other real patient details.
  • Paramind doesn't keep your audio, transcript or record. Everything disappears when you close the page.
  • This is a learning tool. It doesn't replace your trust's ePCR or documentation guidance.
